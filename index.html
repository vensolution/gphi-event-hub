Overtime
Automatic. Any Official Business time before your duty start or after your duty end becomes an overtime row. Official Business dates that fall on your rest day are not counted here. They go on the RD OT form. Check it in the Print Overtime tab.
Overtime without Official Business
Worked overtime but have no Official Business that day? Add it here with the date, time and reason. It is added to your Overtime form automatically and marked “(No Official Business)” so your manager can see why.
